Our Studio

Your local studio, built on care and craft

Welcome to Teddington Ink — a bold, friendly and professional tattoo and piercing studio in the heart of Teddington. With over a decade of experience, our accredited artists specialise in precision tattoos and safe, stylish piercings.

When we opened in 2014, we were a no-frills studio. After a full renovation in 2024 under new management, we've transformed the space into a modern, welcoming and fully licensed environment — built for creativity and comfort.

  • Custom tattoos designed around your ideas
  • Professional piercings with quality jewellery
  • Strict clinical hygiene — sterilised everything
  • No egos. No pressure. Just honest advice.
