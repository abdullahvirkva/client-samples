London's Walk-In Studio, Reimagined

West One Tattoo has stood on Oxford Street — moments from Soho and one minute from Tottenham Court Road station — since 2014. We are a walk-in studio at heart: come in, talk to us, and watch your idea become a custom design while you wait.

Our artists work across fine line, minimal, abstract, Japanese, realistic, traditional, neo-traditional, watercolour and Māori styles — plus cover-ups and blast-overs. We also host guest artists from around the world throughout the year.

  • Free walk-in consultations, no appointment needed for small pieces
  • Custom designs drawn with you — or choose from our in-studio flash
  • Fair pricing for central London, from just £80
  • LGBTQ+ friendly — everyone is welcome here

Signature Styles

From a single delicate line to a full Japanese back piece — every tattoo is designed for you, drawn fresh, and placed with precision.

Fine Line & Minimal

Japanese

Bold, flowing irezumi-inspired work — koi, dragons, florals and large-scale compositions.

Traditional

Classic American traditional and neo-traditional — bold lines, rich colour, timeless motifs.

Cover-Ups

Old ink, reborn. Clever blast-overs and full cover-ups that turn regrets into centrepieces.
