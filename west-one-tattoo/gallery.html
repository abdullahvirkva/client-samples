Gallery & Services

Every piece is designed for the person wearing it — drawn fresh in the studio, in the style that suits your idea best. Larger pieces are booked with the artist; small pieces can be done as walk-ins.

What We Do

Our artists are fluent in a wide range of styles. Bring your own idea, or choose from the flash designs waiting in the studio.

01

Fine Line & Minimal

Hairline-thin linework, micro tattoos, tiny symbols and single-needle detail — our signature walk-in style.

02

Japanese

Flowing large-scale work in the irezumi tradition — koi, dragons, florals, sleeves and back pieces.

03

Traditional

American traditional and neo-traditional — bold outlines, solid colour and designs that age beautifully.

04

Realistic

Black-and-grey realism with soft shading — portraits, animals and photographic detail.

05

Abstract & Watercolour

Painterly washes, brushstroke effects and abstract compositions for something truly one-of-a-kind.

06

Māori & Tribal

Respectful, bold blackwork patterns — researched and drawn to sit perfectly on the body.

07

Cover-Ups & Blast-Overs

Old tattoos reworked into something you'll love — from clever disguises to full dramatic blast-overs.

08

Custom Design

Your idea, drawn with you in the studio. We design while you wait — free consultation, always.

09

Guest Artists

Visiting artists from around the world take guest spots throughout the year — ask us who's next.
