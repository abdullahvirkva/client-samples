QUICK ANSWERS

Before you ask.

How much does a tattoo cost?

Prices vary by artist, size and placement — the shop minimum is £50. The fastest way to get an estimate is WhatsApp us with your idea and placement.

Do I need a deposit?

Yes — a deposit secures your appointment and comes off the final price. Deposits are non-refundable; 48 hours notice is needed to reschedule.

Are walk-ins welcome?

Yes, on a first-come first-served basis. For larger custom pieces we recommend booking ahead on WhatsApp.

How do I look after my new tattoo?

Keep the bandage on 2+ hours, wash gently, moisturise daily for two weeks — no swimming, saunas or direct sun while it heals.
