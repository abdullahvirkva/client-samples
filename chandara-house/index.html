Our Story

A new chapter for a Lynden favorite.

A new family, the same warm welcome.

Our culinary journey began in Thailand, where we cooked heartfelt meals for our family and friends. After running a restaurant near Seattle, a road trip to Lynden stole our hearts — the lush fields, the tight-knit community, the genuine warmth of the people.

We recently took over Chandara House and completely refreshed the menu with our own authentic family recipes. The name on the door is the same, but the food and the smiles inside are brand new.

"Every sauce, curry paste, and dish is made from our family's authentic recipes — just like back home."
