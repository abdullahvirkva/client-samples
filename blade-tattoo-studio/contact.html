Good to know

Common questions

How much are tattoos?

Pricing depends on size, detail and placement — every piece is quoted individually so you know the full cost up front. Wedding tattoos start from £65. Message us your idea on WhatsApp for an honest estimate.

How do I book a tattoo?

Message us on WhatsApp (+44 7956 291350) or use the booking form above. We'll arrange a free consultation, design your piece, then book your session.

Do you offer custom tattoo designs?

Yes — custom work is what we do. Bring your ideas, reference images or rough sketches and we'll draw a one-off design made for you.

Do you do wedding tattoos?

Yes. Matching couple tattoos, wedding dates, initials, fine-line designs and small bespoke artwork for brides, grooms, guests and wedding parties — from £65. Book early, wedding sessions fill up fast.

Do I need to be 18?

Yes — we tattoo clients aged 18 and over only, and we may ask for ID.
