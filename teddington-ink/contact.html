Good to Know

Questions, answered

Everything first-timers usually ask before booking.

Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?

We recommend booking a free consultation first so an artist can plan your design properly — message us on WhatsApp and we'll find a time that suits you.

How much will my tattoo cost?

Every piece is quoted individually based on size, detail and placement. Send your idea on WhatsApp for an honest, no-obligation quote — no upselling, ever.

Is the studio licensed and hygienic?

Yes — we're a fully licensed studio. We follow strict clinical hygiene protocols, sterilise everything and use single-use needles as standard.

I'm nervous — it's my first tattoo / piercing.

Most of our clients were nervous too. We're known for a calm, welcoming vibe and honest advice — come for a chat first and decide in your own time.

What piercing jewellery do you use?

Only top-tier, body-safe jewellery — and we'll guide you through aftercare so everything heals beautifully.
