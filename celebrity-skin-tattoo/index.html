About the Studio

A private, cozy studio with a conscience

Celebrity Skin Custom Tattoos is a private, cozy and friendly tattoo studio in the heart of Camden Town, owned by tattoo artist Maury Decay.

Everything we use for every tattoo is plant-based, cruelty-free, plastic-free and biodegradable — no exceptions. From inks and aftercare to stencil paper, razors and even the snacks, the whole studio is vegan.

  • Watercolour, sketchy, abstract & fine line
  • Blackwork, blackouts & traditional
  • Custom one-of-one designs — never flash off the wall
  • All genders, bodies and skin tones welcome
