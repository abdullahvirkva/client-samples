Good to Know

Before you book

How old do I have to be?

You must be 18 or older — it's illegal in the UK to tattoo under-18s. Please bring a valid ID with you.

How are tattoos priced?

Prices vary depending on the final design, style, size and body area. Message us your idea for an honest quote.

Will I see my design before the appointment?

Every artist works differently — your design begins once a non-refundable deposit is made, and is typically ready to view on the day of your appointment.

Can I reschedule?

Yes — once, without losing your deposit, as long as it's at least 7 days before your appointment and within 6 months of the original date.

Do you offer free touch-ups?

Yes, for tattoos done at the studio, booked within 3 months of your original date. (Fingers, hands, feet, elbows, head and a few other areas excluded.)

Do you do piercings?

No — but our friends at Gold Standard Piercings on the same floor come highly recommended.

What should I do before my appointment?

Get a good night's rest, stay hydrated, and don't come on an empty stomach. Don't shave the area — trimming with clippers is fine.
